# v1.5.0 — Meter calibration

Two visual bugs fixed:

- **Fan meter** showed yellow at idle. Bands were anchored to absolute max RPM, but Intel Mac fans idle well above 30 % of max (the 27" iMac idles at 44 % of max). Bands now anchor to the fan's *usable range* (`min..max`) — idle reads as 0 % effort and the meter is empty when the fan is at its floor.
- **Disk I/O meter** had no green segments. With range 0–3 GB/s the top of segment 0 landed at 333 MB/s, well past the 10 MB/s cool threshold, so the bottom segment was always classified as hot. Shrunk to 0–1 GB/s with cool widened to ≤ 200 MB/s. Common workloads now render in green; > 1 GB/s clips as critical.

Pure-data change; no helper or wire-protocol changes.

213 tests green (86 Swift + 127 TS).
